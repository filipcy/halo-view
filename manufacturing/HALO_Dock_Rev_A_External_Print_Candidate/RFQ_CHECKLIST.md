# RFQ checklist — HALO Dock Rev A external print candidate

Use this checklist for each quotation. A checked RFQ confirms understanding, not release to print.

## Package control

- [ ] Quote references **HALO Dock Rev A External Print Candidate, Sprint 3**.
- [ ] Every received filename is listed and matched to the part manifest.
- [ ] STEP and STL are both retained; STEP is the authoritative dimensional source.
- [ ] Supplier confirms millimetre units and exactly 100% scale.
- [ ] Supplier confirms **no automatic mesh repair, healing, remeshing, wall thickening, or geometry alteration**.
- [ ] Supplier agrees to stop and report any preflight disagreement between STEP and STL.

## Process quotation

- [ ] Process: FFF/FDM.
- [ ] Material: matte black PETG; manufacturer/product identified.
- [ ] Layer height: 0.20 mm.
- [ ] Supports: none.
- [ ] Proposed nozzle, perimeter count, top/bottom layers, infill, temperatures, cooling, slicer/version, machine, and orientation reported.
- [ ] Coupon price, setup price, shipping, tax, and lead time itemized separately from full-size candidates.
- [ ] One each of the 0.2, 0.3, and 0.4 mm per-side clearance coupons included.
- [ ] Faceplate corner/lip, side-guide + lower-shelf, and wall-stack coupons included.
- [ ] Wall-stack preflight shows two discrete pad fields and visible open gap around them, not a solid 1.5 mm spacer.
- [ ] Full-size Faceplate and DockBody quoted as optional **PRINT CANDIDATE ONLY**, with printing held pending written authorization.

## Quality and logistics

- [ ] Supplier can provide photographs of orientation/slicer preview before printing on request.
- [ ] Supplier will record material lot, machine, and slicer settings with shipment.
- [ ] No sanding, coating, smoothing, filling, heat-forming, or other post-processing is included.
- [ ] Parts will be separately bagged and filename/part ID will be traceable on packaging.
- [ ] Supplier accepts rejection for unauthorized scale, repair, support use, material substitution, or geometry modification.
- [ ] Quote validity, remake policy, confidentiality, and shipping terms are stated.

## Purchaser authorization

- [ ] CAD owner completed native Fusion rebuild and interference review.
- [ ] Generated STEP/STL set was visually compared and checksummed.
- [ ] Coupon purchase authorization issued by: __________ Date: __________
- [ ] Full-size print authorization (if later approved) issued by: __________ Date: __________
