# Receiving inspection

Quarantine parts until inspection is recorded. Never scale or automatically repair a received/re-exported file to make it pass.

1. Match package, Part ID, revision, quantity, paired STEP/STL filenames, and checksums to the authorized RFQ.
2. Confirm vendor process/material/finish statement and 100% scale; record actual bounding dimensions against the **verified exported** values (currently TBD).
3. Inspect warpage, cracks, layer separation, incomplete/thin walls, support scars (supports are not authorized), sharp debris, and front-surface damage.
4. Run each clearance gauge on the real bare tablet without force; record pass/fail and measured/observed behavior before selecting clearance.
5. Insert the real tablet vertically into the guide/shelf coupon from the open top. Confirm both rails remain parallel, do not clamp, preserve the selected clearance, and allow the whole lower edge to seat on the full pocket-width shelf.
6. Apply the open Faceplate L to the matching real corner, leaving the tablet outside the two open arm ends. Inspect screen-lip coverage, recess, pocket clearance, skirt engagement, actual radius, top/side bezel, and visible face quality. A closed-ring or obstructed coupon is nonconforming.
7. Wall articles: **BLOCKED until exact pair measurement.** Receive the separately named left and right single-solid articles; reject a file containing two loose bodies. Verify equivalent discrete recesses, flat bond faces, real samples contacting pocket floors, a 1.5 mm visible-gap witness, and no continuous shadow-gap filler.
8. Photograph, measure, and disposition every nonconformance. No physical coupon result releases a full-size print automatically.

Full Faceplate/DockBody parts require a separate receiving plan tied to written full-size authorization; they are not currently orderable.
