# HALO Dock Rev A — Native Fusion visual review 1

**Review status:** Corrections required; full-size manufacture blocked

## Native run and exports

The HALO Dock generator completed successfully in native Fusion 360, including recomputation and timeline checks. The controlled coupon STEP and STL exports also completed successfully. These results validate generator execution and coupon export mechanics only; they do not authorize full-size manufacture.

## Visual findings and source disposition

1. **Lower shelf — failed visual review.** The first reinforced model exposed circular lobes below the DockBody outline. The source correction replaces those lobes with joined internal rectangular gussets whose width is bounded by `dock_side_wall` and whose height is bounded by the accepted 3.0 mm shelf height. They remain inside the existing shelf/guide overlap, below or coincident with the seating plane, and do not project in front of the established guide depth. The validated shelf width, shelf height, seating plane, speaker opening, and USB opening are unchanged.
2. **Rear cable route — confirmation required on rerun.** The L-shaped rear-view form is generated by `CutFeatureOperation`, from the tablet-side XY datum through `-dock_back_thickness`; it is a recessed, bottom-open cable channel rather than an added body. It cannot increase the 18 mm installed projection. Source guards retain the edge-derived USB-C position, 18 mm width, 0.30 mm downward relief, and 0.20 mm rear/depth relief, and reject overlap with the speaker, camera, or button keep-outs. A corrected native rear-view section must confirm the intended open channel visually.
3. **WallInterface — intentionally measurement-gated, not redesigned.** The accepted architecture remains two discrete Dual Lock fields with a 1.5 mm shadow gap. Because `dual_lock_measured_engaged_thickness` remains 0 mm, the wall coupons and measured rear recesses are intentionally deferred/omitted. Once the exact mated pair is selected and measured, the existing implementation derives its recess depth and preserves installed projection. No new mounting architecture is introduced by this correction.

## Gate

Full-size Faceplate and DockBody manufacture remains blocked. Rerun the corrected generator in native Fusion 360, inspect front/bottom/rear sections for hidden gusset containment and an open recessed cable route, re-run interference checks, and record verified full-size exports before changing any release gate.
