# Measurements

## Device

Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 SM-X130, bare tablet.

## Measurement policy

Catalogue dimensions are reference only. Rev A may use carefully captured manual measurements and photographs, but print release must clearly identify every value that still requires validation against the physical device or selected cable.

## Important USB-C clarification

The reported **5.9 mm** USB-C value is retained as captured input, but its datum is not yet unambiguous enough for production CAD. Before the connector pocket is released, it must be confirmed whether 5.9 mm describes:

- distance from a nearby edge to the beginning of the opening;
- distance to the opening centre;
- or another local reference.

The selected low-profile 90-degree cable must also be measured before its service chamber is frozen.

## Visual references captured

The reference set includes:

- traced front outline;
- front and rear orthographic photographs;
- bottom-edge USB-C and speaker photographs;
- long-edge button photograph;
- side profile confirming a substantially flat back;
- earlier photographs with a Polish 2 grosze coin as a scale reference.

## Rev A tolerance targets

These are prototype assumptions, not production-release values:

- tablet pocket clearance: **+0.30 mm per side**;
- non-cosmetic sliding clearance: 0.30–0.50 mm;
- visible Faceplate bezel: **6.00 mm**;
- inner screen lip/overlap target: **0.50 mm**, subject to active-display verification;
- display glass must not carry structural clamping load;
- USB connector chamber: open parameter until the exact cable is selected and measured.

## Measurements still required before full print release

1. Exact body dimensions with a caliper or validated manufacturer drawing.
2. Exact corner profile, preferably using a printed radius gauge or fit coupon.
3. Unambiguous USB-C X/Y datums.
4. Exact selected 90-degree connector envelope and cable exit direction.
5. Camera protrusion and required rear relief.
6. Confirmation that the 0.5 mm lip remains outside the active display and does not obstruct gestures.
