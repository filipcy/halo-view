# HALO Vision

## Product vision

HALO is an architectural interface between a person and their home.

It should feel like it has always belonged in the room. Technology remains quiet and mostly invisible until the user needs it.

## Problem

Most smart-home dashboards expose entities, states and controls. They make the user interpret data instead of helping manage the home.

HALO should reduce cognitive load by presenting meaningful status, attention items and actions. The underlying entities remain available, but they are not the primary experience.

## V1 focus

The first release is intentionally narrow: a premium wall-mounted interface based on the Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 SM-X130.

V1 proves that the physical product can:

- integrate visually with the interior;
- mount without drilling;
- conceal and protect the charging connection;
- remain removable and serviceable;
- support the existing Home Assistant dashboard immediately.

## North-star statements

- HALO should never look like a tablet mounted on a wall.
- The room defines the product, not the device.
- Technology should disappear until it is needed.
- Hardware and software form one experience.
- Decisions and actions matter more than entities.
- Quality is more important than schedule.

## Long-term direction

Future releases may introduce HALO UI, contextual insights, ambient signalling and companion e-paper hardware. These are not part of V1 and must not delay it.
