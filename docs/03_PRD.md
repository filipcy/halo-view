# Product Requirements Document

## Product

HALO View V1

## User problem

The user has a useful Home Assistant control centre but no permanent, visually integrated access point. Using a phone or desktop adds friction, while a conventional tablet mount would look temporary and expose cabling.

## Target user

A technically capable homeowner who values both home automation and interior design, and wants an always-available interface that feels native to the apartment.

## V1 outcome

A Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 SM-X130 is mounted vertically on tiled wall without drilling, remains powered, can be removed for service and looks like a purpose-built architectural panel.

## Functional requirements

- Dedicated fit for a bare SM-X130 tablet.
- Portrait orientation.
- USB-C connection accessible from the bottom of the tablet.
- Two-part product architecture: wall dock and visible faceplate/tablet carrier.
- Tool-free normal tablet removal.
- Mounting through removable high-strength adhesive fastener such as 3M Dual Lock.
- No visible screws or conventional tablet-holder arms.
- Charging cable routed without damaging or loading the USB-C connector.
- Existing Home Assistant dashboard usable immediately through Fully Kiosk.

## Quality requirements

- No tablet movement or rattle during normal touch interaction.
- No pressure points on the display glass.
- No obstruction of required buttons, speakers, microphones, camera or charging port.
- No sharp edges touching the tablet.
- Surface finish appropriate for permanent display in a finished interior.
- Geometry must be manufacturable by a professional 3D-printing service.

## Success criteria

- The installed object reads as a dedicated panel from normal hallway viewing distance.
- Cable is not visible from the primary approach direction.
- Tablet can be removed and reinstalled without wall damage.
- Mount remains stable through repeated touch use.
- Prototype survives a seven-day installed trial without loosening, deformation or thermal concern.

## Non-goals for V1

- LED or ambient lighting hardware.
- E-paper companion.
- Custom Android operating system or launcher.
- New HALO-native dashboard.
- Universal support for other tablets.
- Wireless charging.
- Integrated environmental sensors.
