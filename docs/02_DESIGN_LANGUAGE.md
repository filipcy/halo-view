# Design Language

## Character

HALO is calm, architectural, precise and unobtrusive. It should feel closer to a built-in control panel than to consumer electronics attached after construction.

## Physical language

- Portrait orientation follows the vertical rhythm of the hallway: doors, mirror, tiles and wooden slats.
- Outer corner geometry should visually continue the Samsung tablet rather than surround it with a separate decorative frame.
- The faceplate should be visually thin, with softened or chamfered visible edges.
- A controlled shadow gap should make the product feel light without appearing detached from the wall.
- Visible screws, brackets and adhesive edges are not acceptable.
- The V1 finish is matte black or very dark charcoal, compatible with the existing black interior details.

## Digital language — future direction

- The interface belongs to the room, not to Android or Home Assistant.
- Use restrained colour; colour communicates state or required attention.
- Prefer clear outcomes and actions over raw sensor values.
- Motion should be subtle, direct and without decorative bounce.
- The screen should spend more time quiet or asleep than demanding attention.

## Tone of voice — future direction

Prefer:

- `Dom jest gotowy.`
- `Możesz spokojnie wyjść.`
- `Jedna rzecz wymaga uwagi.`

Avoid:

- technical entity names;
- diagnostic jargon on the primary screen;
- excessive success messages;
- generic smart-home marketing language.
