# Product Principles

1. **Quality over time** — no part is released until its purpose and execution are accepted.
2. **MVP is not minimum effort** — V1 is narrow in scope but complete in appearance and function.
3. **Interior first** — the wall, light, materials and viewing angles are part of the product.
4. **Calm technology** — HALO should not compete for attention.
5. **Action over information** — show what matters and what can be done, not raw entity state.
6. **One-second comprehension** — the primary state should be understandable at a glance.
7. **Serviceable by design** — the tablet must be removable without damaging it or the wall.
8. **No visible compromise** — future expansion provisions must not make V1 look unfinished.
9. **Every visible line has a purpose** — geometry, gaps and transitions must be intentional.
10. **Build less, design more** — new features enter only when they improve the user experience.

## Scope discipline

Ideas such as ambient lighting, e-paper, HALO UI and intelligence are retained as future opportunities. They do not enter V1 unless required to make the core hardware viable.
