# EPIC-001 — HALO View Hardware MVP

**Status:** IN PROGRESS

## Objective

Deliver a premium, wall-mounted and serviceable HALO View for the Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 SM-X130, ready for installation and real-world testing.

## Scope

- Validated tablet and cable measurements.
- HALO Dock Rev A.
- HALO Faceplate Rev A.
- No-drill Dual Lock mounting.
- Protected and visually concealed USB-C routing.
- Prototype, print release, installation and seven-day test.

## Out of scope

- LED hardware.
- HALO-native UI.
- E-paper companion.
- Support for other tablets.

## User stories

- [ ] US-001 — Capture production measurements
- [ ] US-002 — Design the HALO Dock
- [ ] US-003 — Design the HALO Faceplate
- [ ] US-004 — Validate prototype fit and installation

## Definition of Done

- Full-size printed assembly is installed on the target tiled wall.
- Tablet is stable during normal touch interaction.
- Cable and mounting system are not visible from normal viewing positions.
- Tablet can be removed and reinstalled without damage.
- Seven-day field test completes without loosening, deformation or thermal concern.
- Product Owner accepts appearance and function.
