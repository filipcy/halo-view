# US-004 — Validate prototype fit and installation

**Epic:** EPIC-001  
**Status:** TODO

## User story

As a Product Owner, I want the prototype validated before final installation, so that only a safe, functional and visually accepted design is released.

## Description

Use low-cost section prints where useful, followed by the full Rev A assembly. Validate fit, cable relief, assembly, wall positioning, finish and real-world stability before accepting V1.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Critical corner, retention and USB geometry is validated before or during Rev A.
- [ ] Full assembly installs without drilling.
- [ ] Cable is concealed from normal viewing positions.
- [ ] Tablet is removable without tools or damage.
- [ ] Assembly remains stable during normal interaction.
- [ ] Product Owner approves installed appearance.
- [ ] Seven-day field test is completed and findings documented.

## Tests

1. Complete fit inspection against the actual tablet and cable.
2. Perform repeated touch interaction at the centre and corners.
3. Remove and reinstall the tablet after wall installation.
4. Inspect the assembly in daytime, hallway lighting and mirror reflection.
5. Record temperature, loosening, noise, deformation and cable issues during seven days.
