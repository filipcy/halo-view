# US-005 — Design the USB-C channel

**Epic:** EPIC-001  
**Status:** TODO

## User story

As a homeowner, I want the charging cable to be invisible and mechanically protected, so that HALO View looks permanent and the tablet charges reliably.

## Description

Design the bottom USB-C service chamber and cable path for a low-profile 90-degree connector. The chamber must protect the tablet port from side load and allow cable installation and replacement without redesigning the visible Faceplate.

The connector envelope remains open until a specific cable is selected and measured.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] USB-C remains connected without bending or side-loading the tablet port.
- [ ] No cable is visible from normal front and side viewing positions.
- [ ] The cable exits downward toward the wall route.
- [ ] Speaker and microphone openings are not materially obstructed.
- [ ] The connector chamber is parameterised around a measured cable envelope.
- [ ] The cable can be replaced during service without destroying the Dock or Faceplate.
- [ ] The channel does not create a visibly thicker lower bezel without explicit approval.

## Tests

1. Install the selected cable and verify charging for at least eight continuous hours.
2. Apply light cable movement below the panel and confirm no load reaches the tablet port.
3. Inspect the mounted assembly from the front, both sides and in the hallway mirror.
4. Remove and reinstall the cable five times without damage.
5. Play audio and confirm no obvious speaker blockage.
