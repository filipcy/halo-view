# US-001 — Capture production measurements

**Epic:** EPIC-001  
**Status:** TODO

## User story

As a product team, we want validated physical measurements of the tablet and selected charging cable, so that the first prototype is based on reliable geometry.

## Description

Measure the bare Samsung Galaxy Tab A11 SM-X130 and the selected low-profile 90-degree USB-C connector. Record the device envelope, corner geometry, connector position, controls, openings and clearances required for safe retention.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Width, height and maximum thickness are recorded with measurement method.
- [ ] USB-C position and selected connector envelope are recorded.
- [ ] Buttons, camera, speakers and microphones have documented keep-out zones.
- [ ] Measurements are entered in `docs/05_MEASUREMENTS.md`.
- [ ] Uncertain values are clearly marked and not silently assumed.

## Tests

1. Repeat each critical measurement three times; spread must be acceptable for the selected tolerance.
2. Compare the recorded envelope with a physical paper or simple CAD template.
3. Confirm the selected cable can connect without forced bending.
