# US-003 — Design the HALO Faceplate

**Epic:** EPIC-001  
**Status:** TODO

## User story

As a homeowner, I want the mounted tablet to read as a purpose-built architectural panel, so that it belongs visually in the hallway.

## Description

Design the visible tablet carrier and faceplate with proportions, corner transitions and finish consistent with the Samsung device and the target interior. Retention must be safe, quiet and serviceable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visible bezel target is approximately 5–6 mm, subject to engineering validation.
- [ ] Outer corner language visually follows the tablet.
- [ ] Faceplate does not press on the active display area.
- [ ] Tablet does not rattle or move during use.
- [ ] Required controls and openings remain functional.
- [ ] No screws, clips or service gaps are visible from normal viewing positions.
- [ ] Exterior surfaces are suitable for a premium matte finish.

## Tests

1. Insert and remove the tablet twenty times without scratches or damaged retention features.
2. Operate the full touch surface and hardware controls.
3. Inspect at close range, two metres and in the mirror under hallway lighting.
