# US-003 — Design the HALO Faceplate

**Epic:** EPIC-001  
**Status:** TODO

## User story

As a homeowner, I want the mounted tablet to read as a purpose-built architectural panel, so that it belongs visually in the hallway.

## Description

Design the visible tablet carrier and Faceplate with proportions, corner transitions and finish consistent with the Samsung device and the target interior. Retention must be safe, quiet and serviceable.

The accepted Rev A visual parameters are:

- visible bezel width: 6.0 mm;
- inner screen lip target: 0.5 mm;
- tablet pocket clearance: +0.3 mm per side;
- portrait orientation;
- matte black or near-black charcoal finish.

Front thickness, final corner geometry and edge treatment remain engineering parameters. They must be selected to balance stiffness, print quality, tactile quality and visual lightness.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Visible bezel is 6.0 mm around the tablet, except for any explicitly approved functional deviation.
- [ ] Inner edge overlap target is 0.5 mm and remains outside the active display area.
- [ ] Outer corner language visually follows the tablet rather than introducing an unrelated radius.
- [ ] Faceplate does not press structural load onto the display glass.
- [ ] Tablet pocket begins with +0.3 mm clearance per side and is parameterised for prototype adjustment.
- [ ] Tablet does not rattle or move during normal touch interaction.
- [ ] Required controls, microphones and speakers remain functional.
- [ ] No screws, retention clips or service gaps are visible from normal viewing positions.
- [ ] Exterior surfaces are suitable for a premium matte finish.
- [ ] Faceplate can be removed for service without detaching the wall-side Dual Lock mounting interface.

## Tests

1. Compare a full-scale 6 mm outline/render with the physical tablet both on and off.
2. Verify the 0.5 mm lip against all active-display and gesture areas.
3. Insert and remove the tablet twenty times without scratches or damaged retention features.
4. Operate the full touch surface and hardware controls.
5. Check speaker and microphone performance for obvious obstruction.
6. Inspect at close range, two metres and in the mirror under hallway lighting.
7. Apply normal one-finger touch loads and confirm no visible movement or rattle.
