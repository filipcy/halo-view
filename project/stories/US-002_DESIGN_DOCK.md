# US-002 — Design the HALO Dock

**Epic:** EPIC-001  
**Status:** TODO

## User story

As a homeowner, I want a rigid no-drill wall dock, so that HALO View remains stable, removable and visually clean.

## Description

Design the wall-side structure that transfers load to the tile through Dual Lock, establishes the shadow gap, aligns the visible assembly and protects the charging cable.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Dock supports portrait installation on flat tile.
- [ ] Dual Lock contact areas are defined and accessible during installation.
- [ ] Normal touch interaction does not cause visible rocking.
- [ ] Cable chamber prevents bending load on the USB-C port.
- [ ] Dock remains hidden from normal front and oblique viewing positions.
- [ ] Design is printable by a commercial FDM service without damage to visible surfaces.

## Tests

1. Apply repeated touch loads at all four display corners; assembly remains stable.
2. Remove and refit the service assembly ten times; interfaces remain functional.
3. Inspect from front, both sides and through the hallway mirror; Dock is not visually intrusive.
